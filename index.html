Equipos de Elevacion results

Nov 13 to Nov 19. Arrows compare with the week before (Nov 6 to Nov 12).

This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Messages were 1,370 at $74.62 MXN each, down from 1,812 at $53.48 MXN the week before. Odoo shows 37 new opportunities against 39, 11 confirmed orders against 19, and no won deals against 3.
  • What to look into. Google conversions fell to 22 from 50, on $12,580.88 MXN of Google spend, and the Traveling Cranes campaign spent $2,008.44 MXN with none. Worth checking whether the drop in orders follows the drop in messages.
  • What is next. We recommend testing a higher budget on the overhead cranes campaign, which brought 316 messages at $54.92 MXN each, the most of any campaign.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The semi gantry ad, 'One side of your plant has structure. The other does not,' brought 175 messages at $45.00 MXN. The hydraulic stacker carousel, which opens the chat with a ready-made quote request, brought 79 at $49.17. The electric winch carousel brought 74 at $60.72, and the electric hoist ad, which opens on efficiency and safety, brought 90 at $84.12. Worth testing a hoist ad that opens on a plant problem, since the problem-first semi gantry opening keeps the lowest cost.
