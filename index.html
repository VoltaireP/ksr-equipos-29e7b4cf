This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Messages were 1,812 at $53.44 MXN each, up from 1,318 at $69.48 MXN the week before. Odoo shows 39 new opportunities against 38, 19 confirmed orders in both weeks, and 3 won deals against none.
  • What to look into. The Meta ad account status went to 'payment needed' and back to active on Nov 9, so a payment problem likely came up again. Google conversions were 33, down from the week before. Worth confirming the payment method.
  • What is next. We recommend testing a second ad on the semi gantry angle, since that one ad brought 403 messages at $30.80 MXN each, the lowest cost among the top ads.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The semi gantry ad, 'One side of your plant has structure. The other does not,' brought 403 messages at $30.80 MXN. The electric winch carousel, which opens on raising efficiency, brought 148 at $48.50. The adjustable gantry ad, 'Your team loses time every time a heavy load must move,' brought 105 messages and 15 lead forms. Worth testing a winch ad that opens on a plant problem, since the problem-first semi gantry opening has the lowest cost by a wide margin.
