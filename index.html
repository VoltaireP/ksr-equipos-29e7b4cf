This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Messages were 1,318 at $69.43 MXN each, against 1,281 at $77.16 MXN the week before. Odoo shows 38 new opportunities against 44, and 19 confirmed orders against 26.

  • What is next. We recommend testing more budget on the Monterrey winch message campaign, which brought 45 messages at $39.70 MXN each, the lowest cost per message this week.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The semi gantry ad, 'One side of your plant has structure. The other does not,' brought 143 messages at $57.76 MXN. The adjustable gantry ad opens 'Your team loses time every time a heavy load must move,' offers a crane that installs with 4 bolts, and brought 111 messages and 14 lead forms. The hoist video opens on general efficiency and cost $73.94. Worth testing a hoist ad that opens on a plant problem, since problem-first openings keep winning.
