Oct 16 to Oct 22. Arrows compare with the week before (Oct 9 to Oct 15).

This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Messages were 1,560 against 625 the week before, on $173,901.61 MXN of spend against $55,516.64 MXN. Odoo shows 37 new opportunities against 23, and 32 confirmed orders against 27.
  • What to look into. Cost per message rose to $78.73 MXN from $64.56 MXN. On Oct 19 several Meta ads went inactive after an ad review, including hoist, winch and stacker ads, so it is worth confirming they were switched off on purpose.
  • What is next. We recommend testing more budget on the overhead crane message campaign, which brought the most messages, and giving the new Google product search campaign two full weeks before judging it.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The semi gantry ad brought 354 messages at $37.78 MXN each. It opens 'One side of your plant has structure. The other does not.' and invites a WhatsApp chat; its carousel version added 155 at $48.82. The stacker ad opens 'Your team loses time every time a heavy load must move' and brought 73 at $61.55. The two hoist ads open on general efficiency and cost $72.55 and $101.52. We recommend testing a hoist ad that opens on a plant problem, since problem-first openings keep winning.

Actions taken this week

  • Oct 17: launched a new Google search campaign for products (KS-Search-Productos) with 12 ad groups: hoists, cranes, winches, freight lifts, magnets, drum handling, brands carried, competitors.
  • Oct 17 and Oct 21: added keyword lists to the Google campaigns, including new sets on five search and catalogue campaigns on Oct 21.
  • Oct 21: added new ads to four Google ad groups: exact match terms, B2B buying terms, main product and category, and Indeva and Liftronic models.

  • Oct 20: changed the status of the KS-EquiposDNM- Monterrey Google campaign. The history does not show whether it was turned on or off.
  • Oct 19: turned on the hydraulic stacker carousel ad on Meta, moved from on deck.
  • Oct 19: 12 Meta ads went inactive after an ad review: hoist, winch, stacker, Flexlifting, semi electric, jib crane and big bag ads.
