This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Messages were 1,281 at $77.05 MXN each, against 1,560 at $78.78 MXN the week before, on $122,135.12 MXN of spend. Odoo shows 44 new opportunities against 37, and 26 confirmed orders against 32.
  • What to look into. Google spend fell to $23,434.13 MXN from $51,189.22 MXN after budget changes on six Google campaigns on Oct 24, and Google conversions went to 40 from 48.5. The new budget amounts are not in the change history.
  • What is next. We recommend keeping the new proving ground message campaign running a full week before moving more budget to it, since it already brought 219 messages and 15 lead forms.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The adjustable gantry crane ad brought 162 messages and 15 lead forms at $40.37 MXN per result. It opens 'Your team loses time every time a heavy load must move' and offers a crane that installs in minutes with 4 bolts. The semi gantry ad, 'One side of your plant has structure. The other does not,' brought 154 at $62.44. The hoist video opens on general efficiency and cost $87.36. Worth testing a hoist ad that opens on a plant problem, since problem-first openings keep winning.

  • Oct 23: launched a new proving ground message campaign on Meta and raised its budget from $1,000 to $1,500 MXN a day.
