This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Messages were 1,154 at $83.12 MXN each, down from 1,348 at $83.59 the week before. Ad spend was $115,861.56, down from $149,585.53. Your team confirmed 10 sales orders worth $501,265.80, against 11 worth $271,662.00 the week before.
  • What to look into. Meta lead forms were 20 against a usual 41 in recent weeks, so that count is worth checking before relying on it. The Indeva message campaign spent $5,978.30 for 1 message. Google spend fell to $19,946.61 from $36,904.56, and Google conversions to 16 from 36.
  • What is next. We recommend moving more budget toward the semi gantry crane ads, which brought messages at the lowest cost among the top ads, and reviewing the electric hoist video, the biggest spender, at $132.70 per message.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The semi gantry crane ad brought 220 messages at $35.64. It opens 'One side of your plant has structure. The other does not,' and offers systems from 1 to over 100 tons. The same copy as a carousel brought 103 at $50.62. The adjustable gantry ad for Monterrey, 'Stop improvising lifts,' brought 80 at $67.98. We recommend testing a hoist ad that opens with a specific plant problem the same way, since these ads cost less per message than the hoist video at $132.70.
