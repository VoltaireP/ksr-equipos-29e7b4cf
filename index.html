Equipos de Elevacion results

Sep 18 to Sep 24. Arrows compare with the week before (Sep 11 to Sep 17).

This week at a glance

  • How things are going. 1,348 new message conversations started this week, up from 1,135 the week before, and Google conversions rose to 36 from 14. Spend rose to 149,585.53 pesos from 113,593.62, and the cost per conversation of 83.59 still came down. Lead form sign-ups slipped to 38 from 43.
  • What to look into. The Meta ad account moved into a grace period on Sep 23, likely a payment problem with the card or bank account behind it. If it is not cleared the ads stop, so we recommend checking the payment method first. The new Monterrey Google campaign also spent with no conversions yet.
  • What is next. We recommend reviewing the INDEVA campaign, which takes a large share of the Meta budget for very few conversations, and moving that budget to the overhead crane and Monterrey campaigns, which start conversations at a lower cost.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The semi-gantry ad leads with 190 conversations. It opens on a plant problem: 'One side of your plant has structure. The other does not. Most crane suppliers have no answer. A semi-gantry crane does.' The adjustable gantry ad (132) opens 'Your team loses time when a heavy load has to move and no crane is where you need it.' The winch carousel and hoist video are feature lists. We recommend testing a problem-first opening on the hoist ad, since the ads that open on the customer's problem win.
